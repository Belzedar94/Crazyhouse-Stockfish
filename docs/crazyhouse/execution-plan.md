## Experiment ladder contract

Every P7 or P14 strength test freezes full base and candidate commits, network bytes, book, referee, runner, rule profile, time control, Threads/Hash, concurrency, colors/seeds, bounds, maximum games, invalid-game policy, and stopping rules before results are observed. Plumbing changes receive a canary. A canary is E1. STC and LTC are S3 only when their preregistration and integrity checks hold. A flat LLR is never extended after inspection.

The first external-baseline gate is governed by ADR 0006, addenda 001/002 and `p7-local-strength-panel-v2.json`. Its owner-selected stopping statistic is the historical one-decimal WLD LOS display, checked only at complete-pair batch boundaries after at least 50 games. `100.0%` advances, `0.0%` rejects, and no endpoint after the 2,048-game corpus cap is inconclusive. Pentanomial OpenBench statistics are reported independently and do not alter that stopping rule. Plumbing lease 277 remains invalid with no gate credit; CH-239 corrected only timestamp-prefixed multiline transcript parsing. Fresh lease 279 passed the two-game routing and independent PGN canary, but provides no strength evidence. Before the ladder, adapter timing must run under an outer exact Job Object and a separate verifier must reconstruct every transcript search and recompute all 512 paired bounds; neither requirement waives the clean-host precondition.

PASS requires correctness and deterministic signature, a non-draft public change, completed-success CI and resolved review, merge, proof that the exact winner is an ancestor of `origin/main`, postmerge CI, and a receipt. FAIL receives a public English post-mortem with the full tested identities.

## Publication boundary

Local branches, commits, fixtures, builds, and receipts within the canonical project are authorized ordinary work. Creating the public repository, spending significant shared resources, starting production campaigns, changing the champion, resolving ambiguous licensing by policy, and publishing a stable release require their declared decision gates. G15 is the single explicit stable-release decision and is not implied by a green draft.
